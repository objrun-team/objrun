=== 边际处境标记 + 通用方法(symmetry_projection)===
给的是通用方法/定理,非某题答案:

Method: to build a wavefunction that RESTORES a discrete symmetry generated by R (order m), use group projection. The fully-symmetric (invariant) combination is the group average over the FULL orbit: Σ_{n=0}^{m-1} ψ(R^n x); a PRODUCT over the full orbit Π_n ψ(R^n x) is also invariant (R just permutes the factors). A projection onto a 1D irrep uses that irrep's character: Σ_n χ(R^n)* ψ(R^n x) (e.g. alternating signs for a sign irrep). A construction that is NOT a valid full-orbit sum/product, or whose phases are not a genuine 1D-irrep character of the group, does NOT restore the symmetry. Select every option that yields a definite transformation under R.
