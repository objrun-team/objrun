# Current coverage and verification reports

This directory retains current coverage, source scope, original gaps, unreadable sources, and key verification reports. Experimental facts and counts retain their original values; the relocation on 2026-11-04 did not rerun experiments.

- `current-bench-coverage.json`, `bench-coverage.csv`, and `current-source-scope.json`: scope of the current selection.
- `original-source-gaps.json` and `unreadable-sources.json`: originally missing, damaged, and unread sources.
- `remaining-integrity-verification.json` and `remaining-sample-verification.json`: prior integrity verification and evidence of real-sample recovery.
- `terminal-conversion-verification.json` and `verification-redactions.json`: conversion and redaction verification.

Historical reports and item-level audit content are retained in `reports/` under the complete external record directory; see the [record guide](../README.md). Git storage locations and sizes in old reports describe the state at that time, not the current repository layout or disk use. Successful sample recovery does not establish completeness of all historical experiments.
